Privacy Policy

Your trust is our priority. Learn how Krushi protects your agricultural data.

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Krushi, we collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email, and phone number for registration.
  • Agricultural Data: Location data for weather and images uploaded for crop disease detection.
  • Transaction Data: Secure processing of your orders via Razorpay.

2. How We Use and Protect Data

We use your information to provide AI-driven insights, process transactions, and improve site performance. Your data is encrypted via SSL and stored on secure servers.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. AI processing (like image analysis via Gemini) is done under strict privacy guidelines.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preference cookies for features like Google Translate. You can manage cookie settings in your browser at any time.
